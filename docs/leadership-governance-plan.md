> **Project Coo-Cah | AI-Powered Manufacturing Ecosystem**
> **Factory:** Coo-Cah Kitchen Electronics Factory | **Factory ID:** CCK-EL-KIT

> **Status:** Mobilisation Sprint — leadership slate and operating cadence

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the minimum leadership slate and governance model required to execute the mobilisation sprint and prepare the next 30–60 day delivery wave.

---

## 2. Priority Leadership Roles

| Priority | Role | Why Needed Now | Sprint Outcome |
|----------|------|----------------|----------------|
| 1 | Programme Director / Factory Sponsor | Owns delivery, escalation, and decision cadence | Confirmed decision owner |
| 2 | Regulatory Affairs Manager | Owns NIPC, NESREA, SON sequencing | Submission packs controlled |
| 3 | Facilities & Engineering Manager | Owns architect, site, IFC, and utilities sequencing | Architect scope and site inputs controlled |
| 4 | Smart Factory Core Team Lead | Owns staging, MES, and pentest readiness | Pentest execution path cleared |
| 5 | Supply Chain Manager | Owns ASL framework and long-lead readiness | Supplier qualification gated |
| 6 | EHS Manager | Owns hazardous process controls and EIA technical inputs | Environmental and safety exhibits complete |
| 7 | QA Manager | Owns compliance evidence and supplier quality criteria | Qualification evidence aligned |
| 8 | Legal Counsel / Commercial Lead | Owns lease / acquisition route and submission release reviews | Site decision path active |

---

## 3. Governance Cadence

| Forum | Frequency | Chair | Participants | Purpose |
|-------|-----------|-------|--------------|---------|
| Daily blocker review | Daily, 15 min | Programme Director | All workstream owners | Remove operational blockers |
| Decision forum | Twice weekly | Programme Director | Legal, Finance, Procurement, Facilities, InfoSec, HR | Resolve escalations and approve decisions |
| Executive dashboard review | Weekly | Factory Sponsor | Programme Director + all leads | Review status, evidence, blockers, next 30 days |

---

## 4. Escalation Model

| Condition | Action | Owner |
|-----------|--------|-------|
| Deliverable blocked for more than 48 hours | Escalate to decision forum immediately | Workstream owner |
| Cross-functional conflict on dependency | Resolve in decision forum; Programme Director assigns recovery owner | Programme Director |
| Missed critical-path milestone | Notify sponsor same day and re-baseline tracker | Programme Director |
