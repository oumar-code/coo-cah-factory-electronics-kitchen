# Kitchen Electronics Factory — Regulatory Submission Pack

> **Project Coo-Cah | AI-Powered Manufacturing Ecosystem**
> **Factory:** Coo-Cah Kitchen Electronics Factory | **Factory ID:** CCK-EL-KIT
> **Document Version:** 1.0 | **Owner:** Regulatory Affairs Manager
> **Status:** Mobilisation Sprint — submission readiness pack

---

## 1. Purpose

This document packages the immediate regulatory deliverables required during the mobilisation sprint:

1. **NIPC Pioneer Status** application readiness
2. **NESREA Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA)** submission readiness

The objective is to move both items from planning status into **submission-ready or submitted** state within the sprint window.

---

## 2. Sprint Regulatory Priorities

| Priority | Deliverable | Why It Matters | Target Sprint Outcome |
|----------|-------------|----------------|-----------------------|
| 1 | NESREA EIA | Unlocks civil works and infrastructure sequencing | Submitted or fixed legal review date |
| 2 | NIPC Pioneer Status | Protects fiscal incentives before production start | Submitted or approval-to-submit signed |

---

## 5. Submission Readiness Gate

An item may be marked **submission-ready** only when:

- all mandatory exhibits are attached
- the signatory path is confirmed
- legal review is completed or scheduled with a fixed date
- one evidence artefact is filed in the programme control pack
