    Kitchen Electronics Factory — Two-Week Mobilisation Sprint¶

    Project Coo-Cah | AI-Powered Manufacturing Ecosystem Factory: Coo-Cah Kitchen Electronics Factory | Factory ID: CCK-EL-KIT Document Version: 1.0 | Owner: Programme Director / PMO Status: Active — Gate 1 close-out and immediate pre-Gate 2 mobilisation

    1. Purpose¶

    This document converts the current Gate 1 and immediate pre-Gate 2 priorities into a two-week mobilisation sprint focused on unblocked work only. The sprint is designed to turn existing planning documentation into executable packages with clear owners, target dates, dependencies, evidence, and decision points.

    The sprint is deliberately scoped to mobilisation, submission readiness, and governance. It does not include machine-dependent commissioning, production go-live, or blocked Gate 3 work.

    2. Sprint Objectives¶

    Objective Outcome
    Close fast non-machine dependencies Regulatory packs, architect/IFC scope, pentest kickoff readiness, leadership/governance, supplier qualification framework moved to execution-ready state
    Run the sprint from the critical path NESREA EIA, site/lease, architect + IFC, pentest kickoff, and ASL prioritised ahead of lower-leverage items
    Create a live control system Master tracker, weekly executive dashboard, blocker escalation, and evidence register active from Day 1

    3. Mobilisation Lanes¶

    Lane Scope Priority
    Lane 1 — Regulatory, Site & Commercial NIPC pack, NESREA EIA pack, site lease/acquisition decision pack, architect appointment and IFC delivery scope Highest
    Lane 2 — Smart Factory Enablement Pentest kickoff, staging readiness, environment ownership, IFC/BIM handover requirements High
    Lane 3 — Governance & Procurement Control Programme control pack, leadership hiring slate, ASL framework, executive dashboard, intragroup sign-off High

    4. Deliverables by Week¶

    Week 1 — Mobilise and Package¶

    Deliverable Owner Output Evidence
    Programme control pack live PMO Master tracker with owners, dependencies, target dates, gates, and evidence links programme-control-pack.md
    Regulatory pack drafts ready Regulatory Affairs Manager Submission-ready draft packs for NIPC Pioneer Status and NESREA EIA regulatory-submission-pack.md
    Architect shortlist and IFC scope defined Facilities & Engineering Manager Procurement-ready architect brief with IFC contractual requirements and evaluation matrix architect-ifc-delivery-plan.md
    Pentest kickoff arranged InfoSec Lead Kickoff agenda, contact matrix, staging readiness checklist, and required approvals pentest-kickoff-readiness.md
    Leadership and governance slate approved HR Director / Programme Director Priority roles, hiring sequence, governance cadence, escalation model leadership-governance-plan.md
    Supplier qualification framework agreed Supply Chain Manager ASL framework, critical supplier categories, qualification evidence model supplier-qualification-readiness.md

    Week 2 — Convert to Decision-Ready¶

    Deliverable Owner Output Evidence
    NIPC pack submitted or at final approval gate Regulatory Affairs Manager Submission receipt or signed approval-to-submit checklist regulatory-submission-pack.md
    NESREA EIA pack submitted or in final legal review Regulatory Affairs Manager Submission receipt or fixed legal review date with decision owner regulatory-submission-pack.md
    Architect down-selection completed Facilities & Engineering Manager Bid comparison, preferred bidder, and IFC delivery date locked architect-ifc-delivery-plan.md
    Pentest pre-engagement checklist complete InfoSec Lead / Smart Factory Lead IP ranges, test accounts, windows, approvals, environment owners confirmed pentest-kickoff-readiness.md
    Site lease / acquisition at decision gate Legal Counsel / Finance Lead Decision paper with commercial terms, legal owner, and target sign-off date programme-control-pack.md
    Intragroup supply moved to operational sign-off pending Supply Chain Manager Named approvers, sign-off meeting, activation dependencies intragroup-supply-coordination.md
    Weekly executive dashboard live Programme Director Single-page status view: progress, blockers, decisions, next 30 days programme-control-pack.md

    5. Sprint Operating Model¶

    Cadence Frequency Participants Purpose
    Blocker review Daily, 15 min Workstream owners Remove day-to-day blockers; assign actions within 24 hours
    Decision forum Twice weekly Programme Director, Legal, Finance, Procurement, Facilities, InfoSec Resolve cross-functional decisions; approve escalations
    Executive dashboard review Weekly Factory sponsor, Programme Director, workstream leads Confirm status, evidence, decisions needed, and next-30-day outlook

    Operating Rules¶

    • One named owner per deliverable
    • One primary evidence item per deliverable
    • No blocked item may remain un-escalated for more than 48 hours
    • Critical-path items always take precedence over lower-leverage documentation work

    6. Work Explicitly Deferred¶

    The following remain out of scope for this sprint:

    • AI platform production go-live
    • Full BIM population beyond architect / IFC enablement
    • Full MES commissioning sensor registry
    • Any machine-dependent commissioning activity

    7. Sprint Exit Criteria¶

    The sprint is considered complete when all of the following are true:

    Exit Criterion Evidence
    Control pack is active and used as the single source of programme status programme-control-pack.md
    Regulatory packs are submission-ready or submitted regulatory-submission-pack.md
    Architect procurement package includes enforceable IFC deliverables architect-ifc-delivery-plan.md
    Pentest engagement is ready to execute without further scope ambiguity pentest-kickoff-readiness.md
    Leadership/governance model is approved leadership-governance-plan.md
    Supplier qualification framework supports ASL completion and long-lead procurement prep supplier-qualification-readiness.md

    8. Immediate Follow-On Wave (30–60 Days)¶

    After the sprint, the programme moves into the next execution wave:

    1. Regulatory approvals actively managed in-flight
    2. Site lease / acquisition closed
    3. Architect delivers IFC model to agreed issue schedule
    4. ASL completed and long-lead PO release prepared
    5. Phase 1 staging pentest executed and findings remediated
    6. Gate 2 readiness activities sequenced for civil works, IT/OT infrastructure, solar/BESS, MES edge node, and long-lead procurement

    For the master tracker and dashboard, refer to programme-control-pack.md. For implementation dependencies and gate criteria, refer to implementation-plan.md.
